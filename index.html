Frequently asked questions

How can I place an order?

Add products with a listed price to your cart, enter your details and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. For other products, use “Ask for price”. The team will confirm the final total and availability.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Cash on delivery is offered for eligible retail orders in Pakistan, subject to confirmation and courier coverage. You can also inquire about advance bank transfer. Reseller orders use advance bank transfer only.

What sizes are the Flora shampoo and hair oil?

Flora Herbal Shampoo is 500 ml and Flora Herbal Hair Oil is 250 ml.

What is inside the Golden Facial Kit?

The six-piece range consists of Gold Mask Peel Off, Gold Whitening Scrub, Gold Massage Gel, Gold Triple Action Cleanser, Gold Polisher and Gold Cream. Ask the team for current usage directions and ingredients.

Are shipping charges included?

No delivery charge is automatically added on this website. The team will confirm delivery availability and any shipping charges before the order is finalized.
